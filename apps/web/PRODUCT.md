# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Barangay residents submit concerns and emergencies from everyday civic contexts. Barangay officials manage intake, classification, review, and response queues while balancing speed, accuracy, privacy, and accountability.

## Product Purpose

E-Boses helps route community reports and emergencies into the right official workflow. Success means residents can report issues clearly, officials can trust what needs review, and AI assistance stays advisory instead of replacing human judgement.

## Brand Personality

Clear, civic, trustworthy. The interface should feel calm under pressure, practical for repeated official work, and respectful of resident privacy.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative AI visuals, cluttered configuration panels, unclear automation promises, and anything that makes officials feel the system is making irreversible decisions without them.

## Design Principles

Make official decisions easier to verify.

Prefer plain-language controls over model terminology.

Surface uncertainty and privacy concerns instead of hiding them.

Keep emergency and concern workflows fast without feeling careless.

Use the existing E-Boses visual system before inventing new patterns.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA readability, keyboard-accessible controls, reduced-motion-safe interactions, clear focus states, and language that remains understandable for mixed English/Tagalog civic workflows.
