## Overview

**E‑Boses** is a web-based civic engagement and community reporting system for barangays. It enables residents to submit validated concerns and emergency alerts with geolocation and AI-assisted verification. The platform improves transparency, prioritization, and response efficiency in local governance, replacing manual and fragmented reporting processes.

Developed at the **Polytechnic University of the Philippines**, it is designed for scalability across Marikina City barangays.
